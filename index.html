Complete IT Solutions Portfolio

From hardware procurement to cloud integration, we provide end-to-end technology services tailored to your business requirements.

Computer Sales & Service

Enterprise-grade desktop systems, workstations, and all-in-one computers from leading brands. Includes comprehensive maintenance, troubleshooting, and upgrade services.

Laptop Sales & Repair

Premium business laptops with extended warranties. Expert repair services including screen replacement, motherboard repair, battery replacement, and performance upgrades.

Server Installation

Professional server rack installation, configuration, and deployment. Includes virtualization setup, storage configuration, and high-availability clustering solutions.

Hardware Solutions

Custom hardware configurations, component upgrades, peripheral integration, and hardware procurement services optimized for your specific business needs.

Networking Infrastructure

Complete network design and implementation including LAN/WAN setup, wireless networks, structured cabling, and network optimization with MikroTik and Ubiquiti solutions.

Firewall & VPN Setup

Enterprise security with advanced firewall configurations, intrusion detection systems, secure VPN tunnels, and network access control for complete data protection.

CCTV & Surveillance Systems

High-definition IP camera systems with remote monitoring, NVR/DVR setup, and intelligent video analytics using Hikvision and Dahua technology.

Access Control Systems

Biometric and card-based access control solutions for secure premises management. Includes attendance tracking and visitor management integration.

Data Backup Solutions

Automated backup systems with on-site and off-site redundancy. Disaster recovery planning and business continuity solutions with QNAP NAS systems.

Cloud Integration

Seamless cloud migration services, hybrid cloud setup, and cloud-based collaboration tools to modernize your business operations.

Annual Maintenance Contracts

Comprehensive AMC packages covering preventive maintenance, priority support, and regular system health checks for uninterrupted business operations.

Remote & On-site Support

24/7 technical support with remote diagnostics and on-site technician dispatch. Fast response times and resolution for critical business systems.
